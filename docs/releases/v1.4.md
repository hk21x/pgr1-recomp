An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its files.
The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.2 or later:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**. As
1.3 and earlier built everything from source, it then shows **A faster way to play**: press **Update**. Your game keeps
playing until the new one is ready, and your saves and settings are kept. Afterwards, **Manage > Remove the old build
tools** frees the old build tools.

**New install:** download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below, put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
`C:\Games\PGR1`), run it, choose your disc, then **Get the game ready** and **Play**.

## New

- **No more 1.4 GB of build tools.** The launcher downloads a package for your disc (the program and a compiler, no
  game code), and your PC builds the game's code from your disc.
- **RECOMP in the game's menus:** the PC settings in Options > RECOMP, and recomp in a race's pause menu, in sections
  (picture, performance, display, game, controls, wheel), with **bindings and more** for the F1 window and **quit game**.
  Not offered in the pause menu during multiplayer or a replay.
- **More picture settings:** SMAA, smooth alpha (cut-out shapes with MSAA on), a sharp scaling filter and low latency.
  **World textures** is off by default and best left off: it can cause hitches.
- **F12** saves a full resolution PNG in the `screenshots` folder beside the game. The mouse pointer no longer sits
  over the game.

## Packages in this release

Every supported disc has its package: Europe 1.1 (`PGR1-E11-package.zip`), Europe and Australia 1.0
(`PGR1-E10-package.zip`) and USA 1.0 (`PGR1-USA-package.zip`). The launcher downloads the one for your disc.

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; split screen has not been tested.
Please report problems on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the
game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (installing).
