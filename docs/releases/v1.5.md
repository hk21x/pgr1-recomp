An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its files.
The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.4:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**, then **Update**.
Your saves and settings are kept.

**From 1.3 or earlier:** download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below by hand and put it in place of your old one. It shows **A
faster way to play**: press **Update**. Your saves and settings are kept.

**New install:** download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below, put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
`C:\Games\PGR1`), run it, choose your disc, then **Get the game ready** and **Play**.

## New

- **Game frame rate: 60, 90, 120 or 144.** In recomp > game, or the F1 window under Game; it applies at the next start.
  The game runs and draws every frame at that rate, at the right speed, with nothing blended in between. 60 stays the
  default, as on the console. A frame rate cap below your choice is raised to match.
- Busy races may run below the rate you choose: the renderer draws on one CPU core, so a busy street holds about 90 on a
  Ryzen 7 5800X3D while menus and quieter roads reach 120. A variable refresh rate display hides the difference best.
- Saves are kept apart from the frame rate: a driver saved at any rate loads at any other.

## Packages in this release

Every supported disc has its package: Europe 1.1 (`PGR1-E11-package.zip`), Europe and Australia 1.0
(`PGR1-E10-package.zip`) and USA 1.0 (`PGR1-USA-package.zip`). The launcher downloads the one for your disc.

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; split screen has not been tested, and above 60 replays
have not been checked.
Please report problems on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the
game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (installing).
