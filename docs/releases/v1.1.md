An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox, PAL).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game. New here? Download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below and
follow the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp#readme). Already playing? Press **Update** in the launcher,
then **Play**: your saves, settings and achievements are kept.

## Fixed

- **Crash loading a New York race.** After another race in the same session (for example a Quick Race in San
  Francisco, then a Kudos Challenge style challenge in New York), the game could close while loading. New York's areas
  are the game's largest (up to 23 MB) and need one unbroken block of memory; the port's memory pool could leave its
  free space in pieces depending on what loaded first. It now fills the smallest gap that fits each request, so the
  large areas stay whole.

## New

- **Race HUD laid out at 16:9 on wide screens.** The console game is 4:3 only. On 16:9 and ultrawide screens the race
  HUD's panels now sit out towards the sides as in a 16:9 layout (time, targets, position and lap on the left; the
  Kudos panel and the speedometer on the right), each at its true shape, instead of all of it in the centre 4:3 area. Menus
  stay 4:3 in the centre; 4:3 screens and split screen are unchanged.
- **Sharp text and HUD.** The menus', HUD's and text's pictures are enlarged two or four times with clean edges, so
  lettering, the speedometer and logos stay crisp at high resolutions and render scales. On by default; F1 > Graphics >
  Sharp text and HUD turns it off.

## For problem reports

- `XBE_MEM_LOG=1` logs the game's large memory blocks as races load and end, and a failed allocation now lists the
  largest free gaps (see [WINDOWS.md](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp/blob/main/WINDOWS.md)).

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; system link and split screen have not been
tested; the PAL release is the only one checked.
Please report problems with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (building).
