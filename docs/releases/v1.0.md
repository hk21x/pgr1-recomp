The first release of the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox, PAL).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Getting started

1. Download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own (for example `C:\Games\PGR1`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**.
   It downloads about 1.4 GB of portable build tools and builds the game (30 to 90 minutes the first time,
   about 15 GB free needed; nothing is installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. The first start sets up the emulated hard disk (about 90 seconds of black screen and a short
   movie). In the game, **F1** (or **Back + Start** on a controller) opens the PC settings.

Full instructions, building from source, and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp#readme).

## What's in 1.0

- The game: movies, menus, the Kudos Challenge, Quick Race, Arcade Race, Time Attack, saves, sound, the radio.
- 60 frames a second in races and menus, as on the console.
- Direct3D 12 or 11, any resolution, borderless/fullscreen/window, vsync and frame cap.
- Widescreen and ultrawide: the console game is 4:3 only; the 3D view widens to the screen while the HUD and menus
  stay 4:3 in the centre.
- Render scale (25% to 200%), MSAA 2x/4x/8x or FXAA, anisotropic filtering, field of view, hide HUD, skip intro,
  pause on focus loss.
- Xbox, PlayStation and Switch controllers, keyboard, remapping, steering wheels with force feedback.
- 31 achievements with pop-ups (every Kudos Challenge level, golds, Kudos totals, cars, race moments); the list is in
  [ACHIEVEMENTS.md](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp/blob/main/ACHIEVEMENTS.md).

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; system link and split screen have not been
tested; the PAL release is the only one checked.
Please report problems with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (building).
