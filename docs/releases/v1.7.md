An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its files.
The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.4 or later:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**, then
**Update**. Your saves and settings are kept.

**From 1.3 or earlier:** download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below by hand and put it in place of your old one. It shows **A
faster way to play**: press **Update**. Your saves and settings are kept.

**New install:** download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below, put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
`C:\Games\PGR1`), run it, choose your disc, then **Get the game ready** and **Play**.

## New

- **Faster in busy races.** The renderer now prepares and records each frame's drawing on two threads side by side. On a
  Ryzen 7 5800X3D a race at a game frame rate of 144 now holds 144, where busy streets held about 90 to 125 before.
- **16:10 screens filled.** On 1280x800, 1920x1200 and similar screens (the Steam Deck among them), with aspect ratio
  "From the screen's shape", the game fills the screen instead of showing 16:9 with bars above and below. The race HUD
  sits out towards the sides, as it does at 16:9.
- **Aspect ratio changes in play.** Switching between 16:9 and Fill Screen in F1 now applies at once in a race; 4:3
  against wide still applies at the next start.

## Fixed

- On wide screens, a thin bright seam where the menus' leather margins meet the menu picture.
- The settings window (F1) stays on screen when the resolution changes while it is open.
- Shared renderer fixes from the other ports, among them lines drawn one console pixel wide. The renderer's pipeline
  cache is rebuilt once on the first start, which can make that start a little slower.

## Packages in this release

Europe 1.1 (`PGR1-E11-package.zip`), Europe and Australia 1.0 (`PGR1-E10-package.zip`), USA 1.0 and Asia
(`PGR1-USA-package.zip`) and Japan (`PGR1-JPN-package.zip`). The launcher downloads the one for your disc.

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; split screen has not been tested, and above 60 replays
have not been checked; Japanese text on the Japan disc (it plays in English).
Please report problems on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the
game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (installing).
