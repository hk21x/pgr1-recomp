An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing (original Xbox, PAL).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game. New here? Download **PGR1Launcher.exe** below and
follow the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp#readme).

**Already playing? Download PGR1Launcher.exe below by hand this once** and put it in place of your old one (in the
same folder, beside its `PGR1` folder). From this version on the launcher checks for new releases when it starts and
offers to update itself (**Update the launcher**, **What's new**, **Later**), so this is the last time you need to
download it yourself. It then offers to build this version of the game; your saves, settings and achievements are kept.

## Fixed

- **Steering wheels steer as on the console.** A wheel had a dead zone around the centre and a bent response whatever
  the Wheel tab said: the game asks each controller what it is and gives anything but a wheel its gamepad stick dead
  zone and curve, and the port never answered, so every wheel was treated as a gamepad. Wheels are now reported as
  wheels and the game reads their steering directly. Gamepads are unchanged.

- **Squashed car at car select on wide screens.** The more the screen's width exceeded 4:3 the narrower the car looked
  (reported on ultrawide). The car is drawn into a square picture first, and the widescreen widening was wrongly applied
  to that camera too. It now has its proper shape at any width.

- **Ultrawide feedback** ([issue 4](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr1-recomp/issues/4)):
  - "From the screen's shape" now follows an ultrawide screen instead of stopping at 16:9.
  - The race intro's black bars fit the screen: thinner at 16:9, none at 21:9, where the picture was a thin strip.
  - The menu backdrop is no longer stretched: the menus keep their true shape, with black at the sides.
  - MSAA and FXAA are now separate settings and can be used together.
- **Music drowned out in races.** The engine and effects were so loud the mix reached full scale. They now play lower
  against the radio's music, and the mix no longer distorts. **Radio music volume** (F1 > Game, 150% by default; 100%
  is the game's own balance) sets it.

## New

- **The launcher updates itself.** At start it compares itself with the latest release on GitHub and offers the new
  launcher (checked against GitHub's SHA-256 before it is swapped in). A launcher builds the game from its own
  release, and offers to rebuild a game built by another version (keeping saves and settings).
- **Community and support:** the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), linked from the README.

## Also in this release

- Shared runtime fixes from the other ports: the port's own drawing buffers now sit outside the game's 64 MB, leaving
  the game all of its memory as on the console.

Known: the game's sound effects (reverb, filters) are not modelled; system link and split screen have not been
tested; the PAL release is the only one checked.
Please report problems on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR1\play\logs\pgr1.log` (the
game) or `PGR1\launcher.log` (building).
